## Overview

**Avatar: The Last Airbender** is an animated fantasy series that aired on *Nickelodeon* from 2005 to 2008. It follows Aang, a 12-year-old Airbender who must master all four elements to end a century-long war.

## Main Characters

1. **Aang** – the Avatar and last Airbender
2. **Katara** – a Water Tribe waterbender
3. **Sokka** – a warrior and strategist
4. **Toph** – the greatest earthbender in the world
5. **Zuko** – the exiled Fire Nation prince

## The Four Nations

### Water Tribes
Healers and storytellers of the polar regions.

### Earth Kingdom
The largest nation, known for resilience.

### Fire Nation
Powerful, and the source of the war.

### Air Nomads
Peaceful monks, and Aang's people.
